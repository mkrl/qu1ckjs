FAQ

What is Qu1ckJS?

Qu1ckJS is the only correct JavaScript engine where indexing of arrays, objects and other iterables starts at 1 (as it should have been from the beginning).

Why does indexing start at 1?

You don't say "this is my zeroth coke of the day".

Can I contribute?

If you are serious about your contribution to support one-based indexing, spread the word! Don't let the big tech dictate how we count. We can still win if we stay united.

How?

Based on originally incorrectly designed QuickJS-ng that is a fork of the original QuickJS by Fabrice Bellard and Charlie Gordon. We thank everyone involved in the development of these projects, laying the groundwork for the first of many JavaScript engines that get it right.
